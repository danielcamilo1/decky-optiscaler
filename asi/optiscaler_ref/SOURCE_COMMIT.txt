These headers MUST match the OptiScaler build shipped in bin/. The layout
mirror the ASI uses is generated from Config.h, so a mismatch means the ASI
reads the wrong offsets - it fails its validation checks and stays inert.

menu_common.cpp is vendored for reference only: it is where the in-game
overlay's own live writes live, and it is the authority on which options can
change at runtime and what OptiScaler calls each of them on screen.

wrapped_swapchain.cpp and FG_Hooks.cpp are here for the same reason, for one
claim each. They are the two sides of frame generation, and between them they
are why there are two frame rates to report: LocalPresent times and counts every
frame that reaches the screen (State::presentFrameTime, State::frameCount),
while FGHooks::FGPresent times the game's own presents into the FidelityFX FG
swapchain (State::lastFGFrameTime), which is one per *rendered* frame. Reading
those two slots the wrong way round would swap the base frame rate with the
total, and nothing about the numbers themselves would look wrong.

FeatureProvider_{Dx11,Dx12,Vk}.cpp are here for one claim too, and it is what
lets the FSR version change without a restart. Each one's ChangeFeature() reads
an empty State::newBackend as "use whatever Config holds for *this* API", and
clears it again once a rebuild has succeeded. That is why changing the version
marks every backend changed and leaves newBackend alone, where the overlay
names the current backend explicitly: it is the same rebuild by a route that
cannot pick the wrong graphics API, and it does not overwrite a real upscaler
switch travelling in the same command.

unordered_dense.h is the external/unordered_dense submodule at the commit this
tag pins:

    73f3cbb237e84d483afafc743f1f14ec53e12314

It is here because State::changeBackend is one of its tables and the ASI has to
find it in memory: the offsets of its value vector, its load factor and the
std::string declared straight after it all come from this header's member
declaration order. Guessing them instead is what silently disabled upscaler
switching once already, so asi/live.cpp mirrors the layout and
check_asi_backend_layout() in scripts/selftest.py holds the two together.
